# YouTube Preview Popup - Watch Videos Without Leaving Your Page

**The ultimate YouTube preview extension for Chrome** - Preview any YouTube video in a floating always-on-top window without interrupting your browsing. Perfect for multitasking, research, and binge-watching.

---

## Why YouTube Preview Popup?

Tired of opening new tabs just to check a video? With **YouTube Preview Popup**, simply hover over any YouTube thumbnail and click the preview button. The video plays instantly in a sleek floating window that stays on top of everything else.

**No more tab switching. No more interruptions. Just seamless video previews.**

---

## Key Features

### Always-on-Top Picture-in-Picture
Watch videos in a native floating window that stays visible while you browse, work, or scroll through YouTube. The video follows you everywhere.

### Smart Queue System
- **Add videos to queue** without interrupting current playback
- **Insert next** or **add to end** of your queue
- Build your playlist on-the-fly as you discover videos

### Persistent Playlists
- **Create custom lists** to save videos for later
- Lists are saved automatically and persist across sessions
- Organize your favorite content your way

### Playback Controls
- **Shuffle mode** - Randomize your playlist order
- **Repeat mode** - Loop your queue or list endlessly
- **Previous/Next navigation** - Easy controls right in the player
- **Play/Pause** - Full playback control

### Three Preview Modes
1. **Picture-in-Picture (Recommended)** - Native always-on-top floating window
2. **Zen Popup** - Clean, distraction-free popup window
3. **Embedded Player** - Draggable overlay within the page

### Auto-Skip Ads
Automatically skips or speeds through ads in embedded players for uninterrupted viewing.

### Playlist Import
Watching a YouTube playlist? Import all videos to your queue or create a new list with one click.

## How to Use

1. **Install the extension** from Chrome Web Store
2. **Browse YouTube** as usual
3. **Hover over any video thumbnail** - Look for the preview button
4. **Click to preview** - Video opens in floating window
5. **Build your queue** - Use the dropdown to add more videos
6. **Enjoy!** - Navigate, shuffle, repeat, and watch
